      How to play

      Bronson's Big Day
      W A S DMove (arrow keys work too) ShiftZoomies sprint (uses the blue meter; treats refill it) SpaceJump EBark (makes a friend drop the ball and scares off picnic thieves) Drag mouseLook around. Scroll to zoom. C recenters the camera P / EscPause. M toggles music
      • 1Rise and Shine. Wake up and find 8 treats in the yard. A golden one hides behind the house.
      • 2Walkies to the Lake. Lead Mom and Andy through the checkpoint rings to the dock. Tag squirrels for bonus points.
      • 3Duck Hunt with Andy. When Andy's duck drops into the lake, swim out and bring it back to him.
      • 4Fetch-Off at Bark Park. Race Daisy, Moose and Pepper to Andy's throws. First to 3 wins.
      • 5Picnic with Mom. Guard Mom's picnic from squirrels and seagulls until the timer runs out. Catch her frisbee throws for bonus points.
      • 6Sunset Snooze. Beat the dark home to bed. Fireflies add time.

      Your progress is checkpointed at the start of every chapter. On a phone, drag on the left to move and on the right to look.

      Each chapter hides one tennis ball somewhere off the beaten path. Find them to unlock collars, bandanas, hats and costumes, then dress Bronson in the Wardrobe. Finishing the game unlocks the holiday looks, and finishing on Hard unlocks the Halloween looks. Pause and pick Photo mode any time to pose him and save a picture.

      Finish the game to unlock Free roam: walk around the lake as Mom, give Bronson commands, play fetch, dress him up, take his picture, and visit Andy, who is fishing off the end of the dock.

      Pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the title screen. Easy gives more time and slower critters and rival dogs; Hard gives less. Every difficulty has its own leaderboard tab. Finish the game once to unlock chapter select for practice.

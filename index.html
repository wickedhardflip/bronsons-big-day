  • 1Rise and Shine. Wake up and find 8 treats in the yard. A golden one hides behind the house.
  • 2Walkies to the Lake. Lead Mom and Dad through the checkpoint rings to the dock. Tag squirrels for bonus points.
  • 3Fetch-Off at Bark Park. Race Daisy, Moose and Pepper to Dad's throws. First to 3 wins.
  • 4Sunset Snooze. Beat the dark home to bed. Fireflies add time.

Your progress is checkpointed at the start of every chapter. On a phone, drag on the left to move and on the right to look.
